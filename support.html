Frequently asked questions

Where is my dive data stored?

Only on your device. GoDrift has no account system and no server. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I import dives from my Shearwater dive computer?

Pair the dive computer over Bluetooth from within GoDrift and sync after your dive. The transfer goes directly between your dive computer and your phone.

Will I lose my log if I delete the app or change phones?

Data lives on the device, so deleting the app deletes the log. Export your dive log before deleting the app or changing phones.
